# FATE: INTO THE PITS

**FATE: INTO THE PITS** is a text-based cyberpunk management game set in the flooded city of Saltmark.

You run an underground fight club in the Trench, recruiting fighters, dealing with the people around your club, and deciding when to put your fighters in the ring.

The story unfolds through short events and decisions. Your choices shape the roster, relationships, and events that follow, while Fight Nights introduce an element that is outside the game's predetermined story.

## Market-Driven Fights

Fight Night and Side Bet are connected to **DreamDEX Event Contracts**.

When you put a fighter on the line, their result is not simply generated by the game. The player backs an outcome through a real Event Contract, and the eventual market result determines what happens in the game.

This creates the core idea behind FATE: the game's story can be shaped by events happening outside the game itself.

DreamDEX provides the outcome. **Into the Pits turns that outcome into gameplay.**

## The Game

The game is intentionally small and text-driven. Players move through events, recruit fighters, manage their roster, and take risks in Fight Nights.

Fight Night results affect a fighter's record and the player's overall run. Losing streaks can end a run, while winning streaks can earn Fate rewards. A global leaderboard tracks Fight Night performance.

The game is built as a lightweight web application, with the game itself contained in a single HTML page and the DreamDEX integration handled separately.

## Project

*FATE: INTO THE PITS* is the first game in the FATE series and is being developed for the **Somnia Event Contracts Hackathon**.
